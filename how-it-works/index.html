How it works

We learn your process first. Then your technician starts.

Onboarding takes about a week and follows the same six steps every time. After that, every job runs through a five-step daily workflow with your supervisor in the loop.

The first week

Six steps from kickoff to your first job.

  1. Kickoff call

    We schedule a conference call and/or a 60-minute screen-sharing session.

  2. We watch your tech work

    iGEN watches one of your in-house techs complete a couple of jobs.

  3. We document your process

    iGEN creates standard operating procedures and process flow charts for your specific process.

  4. Practice jobs

    Your iGEN tech completes a day of practice jobs and receives your feedback.

  5. We adjust

    iGEN adjusts the process based on the feedback from the practice jobs.

  6. Ready to start

    Your dedicated iGEN technician is ready to start.

A note on remote access

For CNC programming, iGEN needs remote log-in access to one of your local computers. Your programs are written on your own machines and never leave your systems.

Day to day

How every job runs once your technician is in place.

  1. Download and review

    iGEN downloads the template files and reviews the job information.

  2. Draft and program

    iGEN drafts the job (CAD drawings, slab layouts) and/or programs the CNCs.

  3. Final checklist

    iGEN completes a final checklist.

  4. Quality control with you

    iGEN checks the job with your supervisor and/or requests job notes for quality control.

  5. Upload and update

    iGEN uploads the completed drawings and G-codes, and creates or updates the job notes in your system or CRM.
