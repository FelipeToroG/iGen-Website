About iGEN

A CAD and CNC team that works inside your shop's process.

iGEN CAD CAM Designers supports countertop fabricators in the granite and stone industry with CAD and CNC engineering solutions: hiring, training and managing remote CAD drafters, CNC programmers and layout specialists so you can reduce costs and improve production.

Who we are

Over 20 years of CAD and CNC programming.

We are a team of highly educated and experienced professionals with years of proven success in CAD and CNC programming. Every member of our team can translate technical and creative ideas into computer-aided drawings and create the CNC programs needed for fabrication.

Every team member can read and interpret blueprints, cabinet prints, templates and customer sketches, and create and modify drawings in CAD/CAM software.

  • Time management. iGEN takes pride in excellent time management.
  • Attention to detail. An extremely high level of attention to detail on every job.
  • Continuous improvement. We aim for the highest quality of technical drawings, layouts and CNC programs.
  • Your hours, your process. Technicians are trained on your workflow and available during your schedule.
