How it works

Your technician is ready in about a week.

We learn your process first, write it down, practice it, then start. Here is the first week.

  1. Kickoff call

    We schedule a conference call and/or a 60-minute screen-sharing session.

  2. We watch your tech work

    iGEN watches one of your in-house techs complete a couple of jobs.

  3. We document your process

    iGEN creates standard operating procedures and process flow charts for your specific process.

  4. Practice jobs

    Your iGEN tech completes a day of practice jobs and receives your feedback.

  5. We adjust

    iGEN adjusts the process based on the feedback from the practice jobs.

  6. Ready to start

    Your dedicated iGEN technician is ready to start.
