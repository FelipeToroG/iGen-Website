## About iGen

iGen CAD CAM Designers provides dedicated remote CAD designers and CNC programmers to stone
and countertop fabricators across the United States. The company is based in Tampa, Florida.

iGen hires, trains and supervises the technicians. A technician is trained on the client's
own process and assigned exclusively to that client, full-time or part-time, and is
available during the client's working hours. A client can start with one technician and
add more as the workload grows; shops that need the capacity run several iGen technicians.

The team has over 20 years of experience in CAD and CNC programming. Every team member can
read and interpret blueprints, cabinet prints, templates and customer sketches, translate
technical and creative ideas into computer-aided drawings, and create the CNC programs
(G-code) needed for fabrication. iGen emphasizes time management, attention to detail and
continuous improvement.

iGen was founded by David Toro (Founder and CEO) and co-founded with his brothers Felipe
Toro and Andres Toro.

## Services

1. CAD drawings: shop-ready drawings created from the client's templates, customer sketches,
   bulletins and cabinet prints.
2. CNC programs: toolpaths assigned to the client's saw, waterjet and CNC programs, with the
   G-code the machines need. Programmed through remote log-in on one of the client's local
   computers.
3. Shop PDFs: detailed shop drawings and PDFs for fabrication and installation teams.
4. Efficient slab layouts and nesting: each piece is placed on a digital image of the actual
   slab, so the shop sees exactly how the pieces fit, gets the most out of the slab and keeps
   the vein movement flowing across seams (vein matching).

Software seen in iGen's published work samples: AutoCAD (drawings), Alphacam (CNC programs),
Slabsmith (slab layouts) and programs for Titan CNC machines. The complete list of supported

## How it works: the first week (onboarding, six steps)

1. iGen schedules a conference call and/or a 60-minute screen-sharing session.
2. iGen watches one of the client's in-house techs complete a couple of jobs.
3. iGen creates standard operating procedures and process flow charts for the client's
   specific process.
4. The iGen tech completes a day of practice jobs and receives feedback.
5. iGen adjusts the process based on the feedback from the practice jobs.
6. The dedicated iGen technician is ready to start.

Onboarding takes about a week. For CNC programming, iGen needs remote log-in access to one
of the client's local computers.

## How it works: day to day (five steps)

1. iGen downloads the template files and reviews the job information.
2. iGen drafts the job (CAD drawings, slab layouts) and/or programs the CNCs.
3. iGen completes a final checklist.
4. iGen checks the job with the client's supervisor and/or requests job notes for quality control.
5. iGen uploads the completed drawings and G-codes, and creates or updates the job notes in
   the client's system or CRM.
